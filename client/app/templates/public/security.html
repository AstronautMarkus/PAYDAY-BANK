Security

How PAYDAY BANK protects your money and your data, and what you can do to stay safe.

{% call measure("key", "Hashed Passwords") %} Your password is hashed before it ever reaches the core. Nobody at the bank, us included, can read it. {% endcall %} {% call measure("security", "Ownership Checks") %} Every deposit, withdrawal, transfer and card action is verified by the mainframe against your customer number before a single dollar moves. {% endcall %} {% call measure("lock", "Protected Forms") %} Every form carries a CSRF token, so other websites can't submit requests on your behalf, and your session cookie is never readable by scripts. {% endcall %} {% call measure("block", "Freeze in One Click") %} Suspect something?
