This Is Not a Real Bank

PAYDAY BANK is a personal, educational and humorous project. It is not a bank, it is not licensed, regulated or insured by any authority (FDIC included), and it holds no real money. Every account, card, balance, customer and testimonial on this site is fictional, and so is the list of countries we are "banned" in.

Please don't enter real personal data, real card numbers, or a password you use anywhere else.

Why it exists: to show a modern web app running on top of a legacy COBOL "mainframe". Every balance, transfer and card payment is decided by a GnuCOBOL binary working on indexed files, while the Flask client only renders pages and forwards requests.
